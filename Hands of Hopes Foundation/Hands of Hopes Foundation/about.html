About Hands of Hope

Hands of Hope is a community-focused non-profit organisation created with a simple belief: everyone deserves to be treated with kindness, dignity and care.

We support people and families who are facing difficult circumstances by providing essential items and creating opportunities for members of the community to come together and help one another.

Our Story

Hands of Hope was established in 2022 after recognising the growing challenges faced by homeless people, individuals and families within local communities.

The organisation was inspired by the belief that helping someone does not always require something big. Sometimes a meal, a piece of clothing, a kind word or simply being there for someone can make a meaningful difference.

From this belief, Hands of Hope began focusing on simple outreach activities such as food drives, clothing donation campaigns and community support initiatives.

Our programmes are supported by volunteers and members of the community who are willing to give their time, resources and energy to help others.

Our Mission

Our mission is to support people who are in need by providing essential items and showing love, compassion and care through community outreach.

Our Vision

Our vision is to help build communities where everyone has access to basic needs, care and support, regardless of their circumstances.

Our Values

  • Compassion: We care about people and recognise that everyone has different circumstances and challenges.
  • Kindness: We believe that even a small act of kindness can have a lasting impact.
  • Respect: Everyone deserves to be treated with dignity and respect.
  • Community: We believe that people can achieve more when they come together to support one another.
  • Hope: We want people experiencing difficult times to know that they are not alone.

Who We Serve

Hands of Hope focuses on supporting people who are experiencing hardship while working together with members of the wider community.

  • People and families in need
  • Homeless individuals
  • Volunteers
  • Donors
  • Churches and community groups
  • Local communities

Stand With Us

Whether you volunteer your time, donate resources or simply share our message, your support can help us reach more people.
