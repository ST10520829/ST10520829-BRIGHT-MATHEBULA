Our Programmes

Hands of Hope runs community outreach programmes designed to provide practical support to people and families experiencing difficult circumstances.

Our programmes depend on volunteers, donors and members of the community who believe that working together can make a difference.

Food Drives

Our food drives collect essential food items and groceries that can be distributed to individuals and families who are struggling to meet their basic needs.

Volunteers help collect, organise and distribute food during our outreach activities.

Clothing Donations

We collect clean and gently used clothing from members of the community and make these items available to people who need them.

Clothing donations can help people feel more comfortable, confident and supported during difficult times.

Community Outreach

Our volunteers participate in outreach activities where we connect directly with communities and provide practical support.

These activities give us an opportunity to understand the needs of different communities and respond where possible.

Family Support

Some families face challenges that make it difficult to provide basic necessities for themselves.

Through our outreach activities, we aim to provide essential items and connect families with available community support.

Our Programmes Need You

Our work would not be possible without people who are willing to give their time and resources.

If you would like to become part of our outreach activities, we would love to hear from you.
