Support Hands of Hope

Every contribution can make a difference in the life of someone who is going through a difficult time.

Your support can help us provide food, clothing and other essential items to people and families who need assistance.

How You Can Help

Donate Food

Donate non-perishable food items such as canned food, rice, pasta and other basic groceries.

Donate Clothing

Donate clean and gently used clothing that can be given to people and families who need it.

Make a Financial Contribution

Financial contributions can help Hands of Hope purchase essential items and support our community outreach activities.
