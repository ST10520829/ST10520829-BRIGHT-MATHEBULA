Giving Hope. Changing Lives.

Welcome to Hands of Hope, a community-focused organisation dedicated to supporting people and families who are facing difficult circumstances.

We believe that nobody should have to face hardship alone. Through acts of kindness, community support and outreach programmes, we work to provide people with basic essentials while reminding them that they are valued and cared for.

Support Our Mission

Who We Are

Hands of Hope was established in 2022 after recognising the growing number of people and families experiencing hardship within local communities.

What began with a desire to help others has grown into a community initiative that brings volunteers and supporters together to provide food, clothing and support to people in need.

How We Help

Food Drives

We collect food and basic groceries to support individuals and families who may be struggling to put meals on the table.

Clothing Donations

We collect clean and gently used clothing and make it available to people who may need additional support.

Community Outreach

Our volunteers take part in outreach activities that allow us to connect with communities and provide practical help where it is needed.
